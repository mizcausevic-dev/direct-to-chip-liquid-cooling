Direct-to-Chip Liquid Cooling

A 34-second orthographic explainer, 1,020 frames at 30 fps. One selected cold-plate architecture. Heat is moved to outside air. It is not destroyed, and this is not a certified design.

Motion is reduced until you press Play.

What the film is showing

Cold plates on accelerator packages. Coolant stays inside the plate. A liquid-to-liquid CDU passes heat to a separate facility loop. The fluids do not mix in this selected arrangement. CDU layouts vary. This is not total heat capture, not a leak-free claim, and not a measured temperature.

Mechanism: U.S. Department of Energy, Best Practice Guide for Data Center Design, July 2024, section 5.6. Read the essay.
